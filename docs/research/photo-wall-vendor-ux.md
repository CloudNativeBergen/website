# How established photo walls work and look

Researched 23 October 2026 for [#1475](https://github.com/CloudNativeBergen/website/issues/1475), part of the Photo Wall map [#1474](https://github.com/CloudNativeBergen/website/issues/1474). Companion to [`photo-wall-vendor-pricing-hosting.md`](./photo-wall-vendor-pricing-hosting.md), which covers prices, plan tiers, moderation tiers and hosting; none of that is repeated here.

Vendors: **LiveWall** (livewall.no), **Kululu** (kululu.com) and **Walls.io** in depth; **Everwall** and **Relivv** as extras. Only the direct-upload path matters; social-feed ingestion is out of scope for the map.

This file records what the products do. The decisions belong to the prototype and grilling tickets.

## Evidence classes

Every claim below carries one of these tags. "Not documented" means the cited pages do not say; it never means the feature is absent.

- **DOC** — stated on a vendor page, help article, blog post, published screenshot or video metadata (URL given).
- **OBS** — seen in the running product without posting anything: LiveWall's free, unregistered 24-hour test wall created at `https://livewall.no/start` (no account, nothing uploaded, expires 2026-10-24), and Kululu's public demo event `gd50go` rendered headless. Where the tag says **OBS (code)** the claim is read from the vendor's shipped, un-obfuscated client bundle (LiveWall `/<wallId>/bundle.js`, Kululu `app.kululu.com/static/js/main.*.js` plus its English locale file). Shipped code is strong evidence of behaviour but not of how it feels on a screen; no animation was watched.
- **INF** — our inference, with what it rests on.

Walls.io, Everwall and Relivv are DOC-only. Their demo videos (YouTube `lgX7HhaU-Z4`, Vimeo `1179512887`) are JS-rendered and consent-gated; only titles were retrievable. No public Walls.io or Everwall wall was found.

## The five products in one paragraph each

- **LiveWall** is a browser-only wall with five per-wall entry points, each with its own QR and key: Feed (guest-facing social feed), Upload ("deposit box": upload, see nothing), Camera (in-browser camera), Wall (big screen) and Moderation. Three screen layouts: Grid, Polaroids, Slideshow. No filters. Caption is text beside the photo. Realtime over WebSocket. [DOC](https://livewall.no/faq/what-is-upload-page), [OBS (code)].
- **Kululu** is a guest "Digital Album" (grid of everyone's uploads, likes, My Uploads with a Pending tab) plus one big-screen layout called the Photo Wall: a hero item centred over the event background with a strip of side thumbnails and a draggable QR block. Optional Welcome Screen collects name, email, consent. No filters. Caption below the photo, 500 characters. [DOC](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/13010961-how-do-guests-upload-photos-videos-and-messages), [OBS].
- **Walls.io** is a social wall whose "Direct Posts" add-on puts a Create Post button and QR on the wall; the "Lens" photo-booth add-on adds camera capture, stickers, frames, AI backgrounds and on-image text. Seven named layouts. Refresh interval is plan-bound (15 min / 1 min / realtime). [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/5642269-adding-direct-posts-to-your-social-wall).
- **Everwall** is a social wall with a two-step web form (name and username first, then post). Fourteen named layouts built around "tracks" posts slide along. The best-documented moderation console of the five (keyboard shortcuts, bulk approve, 20-post assignment). [DOC](https://everwall.com/tutorials/managing-content/how-to-use-everwall-direct-web-form/).
- **Relivv** is a guest album plus one wall layout close to Kululu's (newest item centred over a blurred copy of itself, earlier photos in side strips). Name optional, HEIC accepted, co-moderators by link and PIN. [DOC](https://relivv.me/live-photo-wall).

### What the contributor can see of others

- LiveWall: a wall has a **Feed** (chronological cards: avatar, uploader name or "Guest", relative time, photo, caption below in a handwriting font, download/share icons; owners get hide/delete/favourite, guests delete their own by device id) and an **Upload page** that shows nothing. The organizer prints whichever QR they want and can later redirect all QRs to the Feed. The home page promises Like and comment; the bundle handles a `like` event but renders no like or comment UI. [DOC](https://livewall.no/faq/what-is-the-feed), [DOC](https://livewall.no/blog/changelog), [OBS (code)].
- Kululu: the **Digital Album** is a thumbnail grid with viewer (swipe, pinch), single-heart likes, sort by date added / captured / most liked, download unless disabled; an "Upload only" permission turns it into a deposit box. [DOC](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/14666521-can-guests-like-media-in-the-album), [DOC](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/11363595-can-i-let-guests-upload-photos-without-giving-them-access-to-others-uploads).
- Walls.io: the contributor is already on the public wall page; viewers beyond the plan's live-audience cap drop into "static mode" with an orange banner and must refresh by hand. [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/9036973-live-audience-content-updates).
- Everwall: wall is meant for screens and event-app embeds; standalone phone browsing is not described. Relivv: guests browse the album later and download favourites. [DOC](https://relivv.me/blog/qr-code-wedding-photo-sharing-guide).

Guest identity in LiveWall and Kululu is a random per-device id in `localStorage` (LiveWall `lw_device`, Kululu guest token); the name, when given, is reused for later uploads. [OBS (code)].

## 2. Filters and text

**No photo-first product offers filters.** LiveWall, Kululu, Everwall and Relivv have no preset filters, stickers, frames or drawing; searches of their help centres, locale files and bundles for sticker / filter / frame / overlay / watermark / sepia found only content-moderation "filters". LiveWall's camera crops to the viewport and mirrors selfies, nothing more; its FAQ concedes "Native camera apps offer many features the LiveWall Camera does not have" [DOC](https://livewall.no/faq/can-use-own-camera). Everwall offers rotate, mirror and crop [DOC](https://everwall.com/tutorials/managing-content/how-to-use-everwall-direct-web-form/).

**Walls.io Lens is the only editor.** Stickers (transparent PNG, 512×512), frames (1080×1080 or 1080×1920), AI background removal with replacement backgrounds, Loop/GIF capture, pinch zoom, "Stickers and text can be repositioned and adjusted in terms of size" (so there is on-image text) plus a separate caption field. Organizer can force "Always use a random frame" on every post and choose Square or Portrait for the whole booth. Colour filters and a drawing tool are not documented. Sponsored frames are pitched as a sponsorship vehicle. [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/13111679-make-your-wall-interactive-with-the-lens-virtual-photo-booth), [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/9969899-adding-photo-booth-to-your-social-wall), [DOC](https://walls.io/product/digital-photo-booth). Whether the decoration is baked into the stored file is not stated; the published booth GIF shows framed output and the confirmation email can offer a media download, so INF: composited at submit time.

**Text is a caption, not an overlay, everywhere except Lens.**

## 3. Layouts

### LiveWall: Grid, Polaroids, Slideshow (keys 1 / 2 / 3, F for fullscreen)

Screenshots: Polaroid wall `https://livewall.no/static/homepage/polaroid_wall_lo.webp`, Grid `https://livewall.no/static/homepage/hp2_lo.webp`, all three devices `https://livewall.no/static/homepage/hp3_lo.webp` (viewed). Layout chapter at 2:30 in `https://youtu.be/JlVo0A6du44`. [DOC](https://livewall.no/blog/demo-3-main-features), [OBS (code)].

- **Grid**: justified rows, 200 px base row height scaled by aspect ratio and stretched to the row width, 10 px gaps. **Portrait and landscape mix by width; nothing is cropped or letterboxed.** Newest first; the owner can drag to reorder, and that order drives the slideshow. No captions, no names. In the screenshot: five-across rows of landscape shots on a dark backdrop with a 1-pixel brand-coloured border per tile.
- **Polaroids**: white 200×240 card, photo 180×180 **cropped square** (object-fit cover), 30 px caption strip in the handwriting font, drop shadow. Each new card lands at a random position (5–90 % of the screen) with a random tilt of ±5°, fading in over 0.3 s. Cards are draggable (mouse and touch), raised while grabbed, and their x/y/z/rotation are persisted so **every screen shows the same scatter**. The screenshot shows about 25 overlapping cards on a flat mustard background, captions like "First photo ❤️", "Our monogram" — the organizer's logo was uploaded as a card too. This is the "scrapbook" the FAQ describes [DOC](https://livewall.no/faq/organize-files).
- **Slideshow**: one photo, **contained over a blurred, darkened copy of itself** (so portrait fills the frame without bars), 1 s canvas crossfade, caption as a centred white pill in the bottom 8 %. Space pauses, arrows step. Empty state "Waiting for images to display…".
- **Chrome**: a 200 px right sidebar in the banner colour with "livewall.no", wall title, logo, Markdown instructions (default "Scan the QR code to post photos to this wall"), the QR, optional upload button and legal text, plus a layout switcher; each part can be hidden. Fullscreen hides the sidebar and the owner can drag a **floating QR box** (300 px, banner colour, logo, title, instructions, QR) anywhere. Colour pickers only for background, banner and text; **no background image, no sponsor slots** (dormant `custom_css` / `custom_js` flags). The multi-device screenshot shows the Grid with a brand-coloured right panel holding logo, "Scan QR to send photos to this wall" and the QR.

### Kululu: one layout, the Photo Wall

Demo wall `https://app.kululu.com/slideshow/gd50go` (rendered headless, 1280×720) and the conference mock-up `https://kululu.com/conferences-photo-sharing`. [OBS], [DOC].

- **Hero** item centred, object-fit **contain** inside at most 65 vw × 95 vh with a 10 px radius: portrait and landscape both show whole, no crop, no bars, no blurred fill; the event background shows around it. [OBS (code)]
- **Side strip**: up to eight small thumbnails scattered along the left and right edges, the set rotated every 20 s; toggle "Hide Side Images". [DOC](https://kululu.com/blog/product-updates-february-2026), [OBS (code)]
- **Chrome**: host background image stretched to the corners (black if none; the demo uses a darkened crowd photo) [DOC](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/11365241-what-background-size-is-recommended-for-the-slideshow); circular event logo top-left; **QR block bottom-left**, white tile with magenta border and caption "Scan to view or add photos!" (or "Scan to add photos!" in upload-only mode), draggable and resizable on screen, "Hide QR Code" removes it [DOC](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/11365160-can-i-hide-the-qr-code-from-showing-up-in-the-slideshow-photo-wall); like counter "❤ 28" on the hero; caption and name in a bottom gradient band; "Made with Kululu" credit (removable).
- **Sponsors** = upload sponsor content and pin it; no slots. The pin article does not say pinning affects wall order. [DOC](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/15805863-how-do-i-pin-or-unpin-posts-in-my-digital-album)

### Walls.io: seven layouts

Fluid (default, tiles grow with content, column count, auto-scroll), Grid (even tiles, row count), Carousel (slider for small embeds; 4:3, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16), Kiosk (one post at a time with "3D transformations", "Time to next post"), Bricks (masonry; large images get more space, three densities, image tiles toggle between image and text overlay, background gradient derived from the image colours when no background is set), Map, Billboard (one post full screen, "Time to next post"). "Avoid Cropping: enable to show all post media in full" is the only statement about mixing orientations, so INF default tiles crop. Tiles show username and timestamp (hideable); no avatar documented; how a Direct Post nickname renders is not documented, see the launch-post screenshots `https://blog.walls.io/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/kafka-summit-direct-posts.jpg` and `…/internal-event-direct-posts.jpg`. Branding: header with logo ≥140 px tall or a 1920×200 banner, background colour or 1920×1080 image, randomly distributed tile colour schemes, brand font and custom CSS on paid plans; the Create Post button and QR sit **top-left**; a bottom footer CTA exists only via custom CSS; **Sponsored Posts** are image tiles inserted every N posts (example: 15) and work on all layouts. [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/1734477-social-wall-design-layouts), [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/3074475-bricks-social-wall-layout), [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/11906580-step-3-branding-your-wall), [DOC](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/5574396-how-to-add-a-custom-footer-overlay), [DOC](https://walls.io/features/social-media-wall-sponsored-tiles).

### Everwall: fourteen layouts on "tracks"

Classic Waterfall (posts enter at the top and move down; "photos are small"), Classic Widescreen with leaderboard right or bottom (posts enter from the left in two tracks), Classic Single Post (new post slides in and pushes the current one off), Multi-column Waterfall (4–8 columns), Feature Me (three tracks; image posts more likely to land in the large featured track), Triple Column, Quad Canvas (image-heavy, four columns, new posts push old out), two Chromakey outputs, Webinar/Live Stream, Single Canvas and Great Portrait Column for portrait screens, Clean Slate for custom code. Preview SVGs at `https://everwall.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/11.svg` … `23.svg`. Branding: a title bar with up to ten logos rotating every 10 s, hashtag CTA, event name, positioned top/bottom/left/right; background colour or image; post font, colour, size, tile opacity; **no QR element**; sponsors via rotating logos or Announcements (full screen, lower third, ticker, inline). [DOC](https://everwall.com/tutorials/social-walls/social-wall-design-options/), [DOC](https://everwall.com/tutorials/announcements/announcement-general-overview/).

### Relivv: one layout

Newest item large in the centre "over a soft blurred copy of itself", strips of earlier photos moving down the sides with four strip animations (Linear, Starfield, Cascade, Polaroid), name and caption under the item, text posts as cards, a **draggable, resizable corner QR**, logo and theme colour, no sponsor slot. [DOC](https://relivv.me/live-photo-wall).

### Orientation handling, summarised

Three strategies appear: **justified rows** scaled by aspect (LiveWall Grid: nothing cropped), **square crop** (LiveWall Polaroid, Walls.io Lens square mode), and **contain over a blurred self-copy** (LiveWall Slideshow, Relivv) or **contain over the event background** (Kululu). Walls.io tiles crop unless "Avoid Cropping" is on.

## Observations for the prototype

These are patterns, not decisions.

1. **The phone flow is four screens in the photo-first products**: optional name gate → native picker (camera or roll is the OS's choice; only LiveWall and Walls.io Lens have an in-browser camera) → preview with optional caption and name → progress → done. Nobody collects an email at upload time by default; Walls.io is the only one that always demands terms acceptance; Kululu puts consent on the optional Welcome Screen.
2. **Pending state is where products differ most.** Kululu tells the contributor "once they've been checked and approved", keeps a Pending tab and a pending pill; LiveWall says "Upload completed successfully!" and nothing else, ever. Walls.io says the post needs approval at submit time. Nobody pushes an "approved" notification to the phone; the photo simply appears in the feed or album, live where the transport is push.
3. **Caption is text next to the photo in every product; nothing is baked into the image** except, probably, Walls.io Lens decoration. Limits seen: 500 (Kululu), 160 displayed (Walls.io advice), none (LiveWall). A handwriting font on the Polaroid strip (LiveWall) and a bottom gradient band on the hero (Kululu) are the two caption treatments on a big screen; names appear on screen only in Kululu and Relivv.
4. **Filters are absent from the photo-first category.** A filter-and-text step would be a Walls.io Lens-style feature, which has stickers, frames and movable text but no documented colour filters either.
5. **Tiled looks**: LiveWall Grid (justified rows, no crop) and Walls.io Bricks (masonry with image/text toggle and a gradient derived from the image colours). **Polaroid look**: LiveWall only — square crop, 200×240 white card, random position and ±5° tilt, two-line handwritten caption, draggable with the scatter shared across screens, confetti on arrival. **Hero-plus-strip**: Kululu and Relivv (one big item, small ones at the edges, blurred self-copy or event background behind).
6. **Branding areas** converge on a logo in a corner, a QR block with a one-line instruction ("Scan to view or add photos!") that the organizer can drag and resize on the running screen (Kululu, Relivv, LiveWall's floating box), and a background colour or image. Sponsor slots between posts exist only in Walls.io (every N posts); the photo-first products say "upload the sponsor's image and pin it".
7. **Arrival behaviour**: push products cut the new item in near the front (LiveWall: right after the current slide, max three in a row; Kululu: one slot before the end of the queue) and mark it (confetti, "New!" badge). Removal is immediate in every product that documents it. Pacing settings are per-type seconds (3–12 s); shuffle is a one-time shuffle at load, not continuous.
8. **Moderation** is a phone-friendly card grid with Approve / Reject per card; bulk approve exists in Kululu, Everwall and Relivv but not LiveWall or Walls.io. Moderator access by shared link or PIN is common. Nobody notifies organizers of pending posts beyond an email digest or a badge. Every automatic filter routes suspects into the manual queue rather than rejecting outright, except LiveWall's NSFW filter, which rejects silently.
9. **Reliability details worth knowing**: LiveWall queues uploads in IndexedDB so a reload does not lose them and uploads originals straight to blob storage via pre-signed URLs; Kululu converts HEIC on the phone and warns iPhone users about the pause; Relivv's wall falls back to a 20-second poll when the socket drops; Walls.io caps live viewers and degrades to a static page with a banner.

## Method and limitations

Three parallel reads on 23 October 2026, one per vendor group, against first-party pages only: product sites, FAQ and help centres (LiveWall 40+ FAQ articles, Kululu 33 help articles and 3 product-update posts, Walls.io 30+ help articles, Everwall 25+ tutorials, Relivv 9 pages), changelogs and roadmap, YouTube titles, descriptions and chapter lists. Where pages were JS-rendered (app.kululu.com, YouTube and Vimeo, the Walls.io demo videos) that is stated and nothing is inferred from them.

Two things went further than reading: a free LiveWall test wall was created at `livewall.no/start` (no registration, no upload, expires after 24 hours) to read its settings JSON and client bundle, and Kululu's public demo event was rendered headless to screenshot the welcome screen and the running wall; its form was not submitted. No vendor was contacted, no account registered, no photo uploaded anywhere. Claims tagged OBS (code) describe shipped client code and are as reliable as the reading of it; they are not vendor documentation and can change with any deploy.
