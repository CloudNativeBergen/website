# Attendee-contributed event photo walls

Researched: 23 October 2026. Context: a conference such as Cloud Native Days Norway; attendance and budget were not specified.

## Recommendation

Start with **LiveWall Premium** for a conference with up to 500 guests, or request/assess its **Professional** plan for a larger event. **Kululu Pro** is the other straightforward photo-wall candidate. Assess **Walls.io** if combining guest photos with public social posts, sponsor content, and more elaborate display layouts justifies a higher budget. These are recommendations inferred from the documented capabilities below, not results of a hands-on performance comparison.

Prefer direct QR/browser uploads for the primary contribution flow. Public social posting should be an optional additional source: direct uploads let participants contribute without a social-media account or publishing to their followers.

The target flow is: guest scans QR → uploads from phone browser → organizer approves → photo appears on a TV/projector → organizer downloads the collection afterwards. A shared album alone does not establish that a product supports a continuously updating event display.

## Photo-first products

### LiveWall — strongest local candidate

- Operated by Perceptron AS in Oslo. Guests scan a QR code and upload without installing an app. Live slideshow and a Polaroid wall are available. [Product](https://livewall.no/), [operator and privacy](https://livewall.no/privacy-policy).
- Rendered Norwegian pricing showed **Plus NOK 250**, **Premium NOK 450**, **Signature NOK 650**, each a one-time payment. All list up to **500 guests**. Plus includes manual moderation, styling, password protection, ZIP export, a three-month upload window and one-year storage. Premium adds videos, automatic safety filtering and Google Drive sync. Signature adds embedding, FTP and Zapier. Prices are observed localized quotes; confirm final tax treatment. [Pricing](https://livewall.no/pricing).
- Its Professional selector showed **$75/month for up to 1,000 attendees**. It lists white-labeling, unlimited walls, DPA, onboarding and monthly billing with cancellation; professional prices exclude VAT. Do not treat the consumer 500-person allowance as unlimited conference capacity. [Professional pricing](https://livewall.no/pro-pricing).
- Manual approval can prevent a photo appearing on either the wall or guest feed until an organizer/designated moderator approves it. [Moderation](https://livewall.no/faq/what-is-moderation-and-safety-filter).
- Hosting needs written clarification. The localized pricing page says Norwegian data centers; its privacy policy says personal/usage data is in Sweden/Norway, content location depends on where the wall is created, and CDN caching can occur worldwide. The same policy later says data at rest is in Norway/Sweden. Confirm the actual event's media region and backups rather than promising Norway-only storage. [Privacy policy](https://livewall.no/privacy-policy).

**Assessment:** attractive price and conference fit, particularly if local vendor support matters. Verify branding and attendee-count definitions for the selected plan.

### Kululu — straightforward branded live wall

- QR/link uploads require no guest app or account; photos, videos and text messages feed a digital album and live slideshow. [Overview](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/11402245-what-is-kululu).
- Its live Norwegian pricing widget showed **Free NOK 0**, **Plus NOK 389**, **Pro NOK 969**, with paid prices explicitly excluding taxes. Free allows 100 uploads/24 hours and seven-day storage; Plus allows 500 uploads, one-month uploading and three-month storage; Pro lists unlimited uploads/participants, three-month uploading, one-year storage, full brand control and a custom event URL. Published pricing carries a limited-time promotion banner; recheck before purchase. [Pricing](https://www.kululu.com/pricing), [rendered pricing widget](https://app.kululu.com/pricing-embed).
- **Manual approval requires Pro.** Uploads otherwise publish immediately; enable approval before the event. [Approval instructions](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/11363405-how-do-i-approve-guest-uploads-before-they-appear-in-the-album).
- Hosting documents conflict: the privacy policy specifies US Firebase storage, while the security page mentions US Central and EU Central. A DPA is published. Ask which region and processing terms apply to the event. [Privacy](https://www.kululu.com/privacy), [security](https://www.kululu.com/security), [DPA](https://www.kululu.com/dpa).

**Assessment:** a strong simple alternative; compare Pro against LiveWall Premium/Professional using the actual conference size.

### GuestCam (guestcam.co) — inexpensive collection plus slideshow

- Direct QR/link uploads require no guest app/account. Includes private galleries, moderation and original-quality ZIP downloads. [Product](https://guestcam.co/photo-sharing).
- Current advertised prices: **Standard $49/event** and **Premium $97/event**, beside higher reference prices. Standard includes unlimited guests/photos/videos, six-month uploading and twelve-month storage. Premium increases those windows to twelve/fourteen months and includes six main galleries. Both include the live slideshow; guest-portal custom branding is separately quoted. [Pricing](https://guestcam.co/pricing).
- Moderation applies only to uploads made after enabling it; existing pending uploads still need review if it is later disabled. [Moderation](https://help.guestcam.co/en/articles/9779738-how-to-turn-on-off-content-moderation).
- The slideshow automatically adds new uploads, but only one sub-gallery can be presented at a time. Verify approval-to-screen behavior in a demo before using it publicly. [Slideshow](https://help.guestcam.co/en/articles/9779760-how-the-slideshow-feature-works-and-how-to-start-it).
- "Unlimited" has fair-use conditions: upload counts significantly above 25,000 trigger review, with potential custom plans or temporary limits after contact. Data hosting is described as US-based. [Fair use](https://guestcam.co/fair-usage), [security/privacy](https://guestcam.co/security-privacy).

**Assessment:** budget candidate when collecting photos matters as much as the screen. There is also an unrelated-looking product at guestcam.app; this comparison and its prices refer specifically to guestcam.co.

### GUESTPIX Business — branded gallery with live refresh

- Official business pricing rendered **Bronze $49/event**, **Silver $97/event**, and **Platinum $197 for three galleries**. Bronze includes moderation, three-month uploading and twelve-month hosting; Silver adds white-labeling and twelve-month uploading. The "unlimited guests" offer has a **1,000-guest maximum per gallery** under fair use. Registered charities/nonprofits can ask about a discount. [Business pricing](https://guestpix.com/business-pricing/?currency=USD).
- QR/private-link collection is browser-based; no guest app is needed. [Business flow](https://guestpix.com/business-how-it-works/).
- Every paid package includes a browser slideshow. **Enable Live Refresh** to add newly uploaded media automatically; new items join at the end. Slideshow filters can restrict display to approved items. [Slideshow instructions](https://help.guestpix.com/article/30-how-does-the-slideshow-function-work).
- Moderation holds photos/videos from the main gallery until approved, with bulk review available. [Moderation](https://help.guestpix.com/article/174-what-is-content-moderation-on-guestpix-and-how-does-it-work).
- Content storage is in North America with a US backup; European edge nodes improve delivery but are not EU-only storage. [Storage](https://help.guestpix.com/article/20-where-are-your-guest-photos-stored).

**Assessment:** useful for an event album that remains available after the conference. Check whether its slideshow presentation suits the desired large-screen experience.

### Relivv — additional EU-storage candidate

- QR/browser uploads, no guest app/account, fullscreen live slideshow. **Pro €99/event** includes pre-publication moderation, branding removal, unlimited advertised uploads/participants, three-month uploading and one-year storage. Free and €39 Plus are more limited; approval is a Pro feature. [Product/pricing](https://relivv.me/).
- The Bulgarian operator documents application hosting in Frankfurt and media storage in Stockholm. However, its subprocessors include US-based Anthropic for AI moderation, plus US payment/email providers. EU storage alone does not establish that all processing remains in the EU. [Privacy/subprocessors](https://relivv.me/privacy).

**Assessment:** worth a demo if specified EU media storage is a priority; support, event-scale reliability and processing configuration were not tested.

## Social-wall products

### Walls.io — social feeds and guest photos on one display

- Direct Posts accepts image/text submissions in a browser via QR/link without a social-media account. Manual moderation can hold posts until approved. [Direct Posts](https://blog.walls.io/product/direct-posts-feature/), [approval](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/1617830-manual-moderation-of-your-social-wall).
- The official rendered event page showed **€225 for a 30-day Event Pass**, plus a required **€100 Interactivity Pack** for Direct Posts: **€325 starting total** for this use case. It lists five sources, unlimited posts, one-minute refresh and **500 unique live wall visitors**. This audience definition concerns people accessing the wall, not simply conference attendance. Photo Booth is a separate add-on; it is not necessary for ordinary Direct Posts. [Event pricing](https://walls.io/pricing/events), [add-ons](https://www.walls.io/features/add-ons).
- A separate product page describes screen display, embedding, branding and layouts; confirm exact white-label entitlements on the selected pass. [Events](https://www.walls.io/solutions/events).
- DPA eligibility needs confirmation: older help says Enterprise, while the newsroom says standard. Frankfurt hosting is documented in the older help, but current privacy material lists US subprocessors. Do not interpret that hosting statement as EU-only processing. [Hosting/DPA help](https://help.walls.io/en/articles/5634115-data-privacy-ccpa-gdpr-compliance), [newsroom](https://www.walls.io/press-newsroom), [privacy](https://walls.io/privacy).

**Assessment:** suitable when social aggregation and display customization are central. Costlier than the photo-first options, and the direct-upload add-on must be included when comparing prices.

### Everwall — transparent pricing for an event social wall

- Everwall Direct provides a QR/link web form for photos, text and MP4 videos; media files are limited to 20 MB. [Guest upload form](https://everwall.com/tutorials/managing-content/how-to-use-everwall-direct-web-form/).
- **Full Moderation** queues submissions for approval before display. Ordinary filters or the ability to remove a published post are different features. [Moderation](https://everwall.com/tutorials/managing-content/how-to-moderate-your-social-wall/).
- **$49/event day + $29/day Full Moderation = $78/day** for an approved live wall. Removing Everwall branding adds $49/day, totaling **$127/day**. Its **$399/30-day pass** includes both moderation and branding removal. Direct web form, unlimited attendees/screens/posts and media ZIP export are listed; X ingestion has separate limits. The table advertises a standard DPA on all plans. [Event pricing](https://everwall.com/pricing/event-social-wall/).
- The privacy policy explicitly describes transfers/processing in the US; no EU hosting option was verified. [Privacy](https://everwall.com/privacy).

**Assessment:** useful when public social posts and short-event pricing matter. Compare the moderated/brand-free total, not just the $49 headline.

### Social Walls by Taggbox — qualified candidate requiring a quote

- SnapUp allows guest uploads via QR/link without a social-media account; the product supports real-time screen display. [SnapUp](https://taggbox.com/blog/taggbox-displays-ugc-uploader-for-events/), [product](https://socialwalls.com/).
- Official event pricing material confirms approval-before-publication moderation. The indexed weekly-pass table lists one event, 500 attendees, two feeds and 500 retained posts; event-day realtime differs from the slower setup refresh. It marks content export, collaborators and on-demand DPA unavailable on the weekly plan. **Exact current price was not verified**: official access returned HTTP 403. Request pass-specific pricing and entitlements; do not substitute pricing for Taggbox's separate website-widget product. [Event pricing and FAQ](https://taggbox.com/price/display-price/).
- Current EU hosting and contractual DPA availability were not established from accessible official material.

**Assessment:** plausible feature fit; lower confidence as a purchase recommendation until price, export and contract terms are verified.

## What to verify in a short vendor trial

1. Scan from both iPhone and Android; upload camera-roll photos and an iPhone HEIC image. Check whether a name, email or sign-in is required in the chosen configuration.
2. Enable approval before uploading. Confirm pending photos stay off both guest gallery and screen; approve one, then remove it while the screen is running.
3. Check whether new photos interrupt the loop, join its end, or wait for a refresh. Measure the delay rather than relying on "instant" marketing.
4. Run on the actual venue screen/browser. Inspect portrait and landscape images, captions, sponsor branding and QR readability from the room.
5. Check venue Wi-Fi, simultaneous uploads, and behavior when the display temporarily loses internet. No offline reliability claim was independently tested here.
6. Confirm attendee, upload, storage and live-viewer limits. Attendee counts, concurrent screen viewers and photo limits are different quantities.
7. Confirm full-resolution export, retention/deletion, the contracted media region and subprocessors. For event use, make the intended display and subsequent photo use clear to contributors.

## Method and limitations

First-party product, pricing and help/privacy pages were checked. JavaScript-rendered tables were inspected in a browser where text extraction omitted the prices (Kululu, GUESTPIX, LiveWall; social-wall findings describe their own checks below). Prices are a 23 October 2026 snapshot, in the displayed currency; no exchange-rate conversion is implied. Discounts, taxes and geography can change the checkout total.

No accounts were created, vendors contacted, subscriptions bought or event photos uploaded. Claims about features, capacity and hosting are vendor documentation, not independent performance or security verification. This is a research-only document; no application code changed and software tests are not relevant.
